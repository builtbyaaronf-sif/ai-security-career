# Where to document (ranked by hiring value)

| # | Channel | Why it matters in security | Start |
|---|---|---|---|
| 1 | **GitHub** | Recruiters check code and commit history first | Week 1 |
| 2 | **Writeups / blog** on your own domain (you can build it with FORGE's stack) | Security hires on written findings. A report is the job | Week 4 |
| 3 | **LinkedIn** | Where recruiters search and you get found | Week 1 |
| 4 | **Credly** | Verified cert badges (ISC2 CC issues one) | When certified |
| 5 | **Public lab profiles**: TryHackMe, Hack The Box, PortSwigger | Third party proof of hands on skill and a rank | Week 2 |
| 6 | **huntr.com / bug bounty profiles** | Valid findings in the real world beat any cert | Phase 5 |
| 7 | **Demo videos** (YouTube unlisted or Loom) | 2 minutes showing an attack blocked is stronger than a paragraph | Phase 4 |
| 8 | **Community**: OWASP London chapter, BSides London, DEF CON AI Village (online) | Most security roles are filled by referral. Free to attend | Month 2 |
| 9 | **CV** (one page, built from `EVIDENCE.md`) | The final summary | Rewrite monthly |

Skip for now: X/Twitter, Medium paywalled posts, paid "portfolio" sites.
